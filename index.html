About

I'm Gideon Ajagbe. I started by making websites for local brands, then saw the bigger problem. Now I'm building Handlam, and the systems that run real companies.

I study Information Technology at Anchor University, Lagos, and I build for the businesses around me. I care about software that works on a cheap phone, with bad network, for someone who has never used an app like it before.

What I bring to a team

  • I ship whole products. Design, phone app, server, database, payments and launch, not just one piece.
  • I build for real users. My work is used by traders, dealers, staff and managers, not only class projects.
  • I handle money safely. Live card and transfer payments, two-person checks, and nothing trusted without checking.
  • I can explain and sell an idea. I pitched a digital plan to a company's leadership and got it approved.
  • I learn fast. I picked up new tools for every project, from AI features to offline phone apps.
