My company

Handlam

A shop app for Nigerian traders. Say what you sold, and Handlam counts the money, the stock and who owes you. It works on a cheap phone, even with no network.

  • Record a sale by voice, barcode or a few taps
  • Stock warnings before goods run out
  • Debt reminders and AI money advice
  • Card, transfer and USSD payments
